Investigation

Temperature: I chose this data because it has a direct impact on the chance of the wildfire. The higher the temperature the bigger the chance of wildfire because the tree becomes much drier and can catch on fire much easily. Light level: Light level also means a lot to a wildfire chance because the bigger light level the more heat the Sun can give and due to it temperature can rapidly increase.

I selected wildfire because it is a really big problem in dry regions like in America or Australia and this problem has the biggest impact on ecology than anything else.

There are already a few systems that detect wildfire, for example AI cameras that detect smoke from far away and send an information immediately or satellite wildfire detection that detects wildfire from infrared radiation from heat sources. My system will be different from them, it will use sensors to detect temperature and light level and count wildfire risk.

I've done the research and found that conifers have a greater propensity for fires than deciduous trees because they have fewer needles on their own, contain less water, dry out faster, and can ignite more easily. And they also release resin that ignites very easily and burns for a long time.
